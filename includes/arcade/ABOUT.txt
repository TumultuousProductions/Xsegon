The names of arcade sets
========================

names.tsv.gz is the list Scan for Games reads to know what an arcade
game's file is: kof2003.zip is "The King of Fighters 2003", neogeo.zip is
a BIOS set and not a game. Four facts a machine: its short name, its
title, what kind of thing it is, and its parent.

Where it comes from
-------------------
MAME's own list of machines (mame -listxml), MAME 0.238 (mame0238).

43999 machines: 20261 games, 73 BIOS sets, 5460 devices,
15440 mechanical machines, 2765 computers and consoles.

A newer list: php tools\get-arcade-names.php --mame <a newer mame.exe>
