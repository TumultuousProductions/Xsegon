Xsegon's built-in interface sounds
==================================

Move.wav, Select.wav, Back.wav, Ok.wav, Hover.wav and Startup.wav are the
set a new copy of Xsegon starts on, and the sounds it plays when the set
chosen in Settings > Sound is not on this PC. Move plays as you go from one
thing to the next, Select when you choose, Back when you leave, Ok when a
download or a fetch finishes, and Hover as the mouse pointer comes onto a
game. Hover is also what a sound set with no Hover of its own plays.
Nothing plays Startup.

Where they come from
--------------------
"Fantasy UI Sound FX Pack Vol. 1" by Ovani Sound (https://ovanisound.com),
used unchanged under Ovani Sound's royalty-free licence:
https://ovanisound.com/policies/terms-of-service

    Move.wav      Hovers A 001
    Select.wav    Select A 003
    Back.wav      Back A 004
    Ok.wav        Transition A 002
    Startup.wav   Transition A 002

These five come with the Xsegon download. They are not in Xsegon's source
code on GitHub, where they would be handed out on their own: a copy run
from the source plays the sounds of Xsegon 2 in their place.

Hover.wav is Xsegon's own, made by Tumultuous Productions, Xsegon's maker
(first named "soft hover low pitch 4 xsegon"), and is under Xsegon's MIT
licence like the rest of Xsegon.

What that means for you
-----------------------
The five from Ovani Sound are NOT covered by Xsegon's MIT licence (the
LICENSE file at the top of Xsegon). They belong to Ovani Sound and are here
as part of Xsegon's interface. You may use Xsegon with them freely. You may
not take them out and distribute, sell or sublicense them on their own, and
they may not be used to train AI systems. For sounds to use in your own
project, get the pack from Ovani Sound.

A pack of your own
------------------
Make a folder in Xsegon\data\sounds with three files named Move, Select
and Back (each a .wav, .ogg or .mp3), then choose it in Settings > Sound.
An Ok file is optional: without one, Select plays when something finishes.
A Hover file is optional too: it plays as the mouse pointer comes onto a
game. Without one, the built-in Hover plays.
This built-in set is never changed by that, and never changes yours.
