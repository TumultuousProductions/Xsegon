Xsegon: the parts that are not Xsegon's own
===========================================

Xsegon itself is under the MIT licence (LICENSE). The download also carries,
or fetches on your PC when you ask, the parts below. Each keeps its own
licence; where the licence must travel with it, it is beside the file.

Programs carried in the download, unchanged
-------------------------------------------
PHP (runtime\php)
    The PHP Group, php.net. The PHP License v3.01: runtime\php\license.txt.
    Xsegon is a set of PHP pages; this is the PHP that runs them. Its
    mbstring part (libmbfl) is under the GNU LGPL 2.1, so PHP's source is
    kept, unchanged, on github.com/TumultuousProductions/Xsegon-helper-files.

RetroArch (runtime\RetroArch\RetroArch.7z)
    libretro, retroarch.com. GNU GPL v3. The licence, the pinned version and
    where its source code is: runtime\RetroArch\RetroArch-LICENSE.txt.
    Setup unpacks it into data\emulators\RetroArch; Xsegon starts it as a
    separate program and links nothing of it.

7-Zip's 7zr.exe (bin\7zr.exe)
    Igor Pavlov, 7-zip.org. GNU LGPL: bin\7zr-LICENSE.txt. Unpacks RetroArch.

ffmpeg.exe (bin\ffmpeg.exe)
    The FFmpeg project, ffmpeg.org; the Windows build by Gyan Doshi, gyan.dev.
    GNU GPL v3, with where its source is: bin\ffmpeg-LICENSE.txt. Keeps the
    first 30 seconds of a game's recording as its video.

pdf.js 6.4.299 (js\vendor\pdf-6.4.299)
    Mozilla and the pdf.js contributors, mozilla.github.io/pdf.js. Apache
    License 2.0: js\vendor\pdf-6.4.299\LICENSE. With its picture decoders,
    OpenJPEG (BSD 2-clause), PDFium's JBIG2 (BSD 3-clause) and QCMS (MIT),
    each licence beside it in the same folder. Draws a manual's pages.

StPageFlip 2.0.7 (js\vendor\page-flip-2.0.7)
    Nodlik, nodlik.github.io/StPageFlip. MIT licence:
    js\vendor\page-flip-2.0.7\LICENSE. Bends and turns a manual's pages.

Data and pictures carried in the download
-----------------------------------------
The LaunchBox Games Database (gamesdb\)
    LaunchBox (launchbox-app.com) and the people who add to it. Game names,
    details and which pictures exist; all credit to them (gamesdb\README.md).
    The pictures themselves are fetched from LaunchBox's picture server one
    game at a time, when you ask, and libretro's thumbnails stand in when that
    server does not answer.

Console icons (img\consoles)
    RetroArch's menu icons, by the libretro team and contributors, from
    retroarch-assets. CC-BY 4.0: img\consoles\ABOUT.txt.

Console name logos and photos (img\console-logos, img\console-photos)
    From Wikimedia Commons, each marked Public domain or CC0 there. The names
    and logos are still their owners' trademarks: img\console-logos\ABOUT.txt
    and the README's Trademarks section.

The list of controllers (includes\padlist)
    SDL_GameControllerDB. zlib licence: includes\padlist\LICENSE.txt.

The names of arcade games (includes\arcade)
    From MAME's own list of its machines, by the MAME team:
    includes\arcade\ABOUT.txt. Xsegon comes with no MAME and no arcade games.

The controller outlines (js\padpic.js)
    The Xbox 360 gamepad by Grumbel (CC0) and the DualShock 4 layout by
    Tokyoship (CC BY 3.0), both on Wikimedia Commons; the outline only.

The Dracula themes' colours
    The Dracula palette, Copyright (c) 2023 Dracula Theme, MIT licence.

Fetched on your PC when you ask, never carried
----------------------------------------------
RetroArch's cores, from buildbot.libretro.com, each under its own licence.
libretro's thumbnails, names and cheat lists (libretro on GitHub).
Videos from the Internet Archive, World of Longplays and the Arcade Database
(the README's Credits name them and their makers).
Pictures from the LaunchBox Games Database's picture server.

Xsegon comes with no games, no BIOS files and no keys.
